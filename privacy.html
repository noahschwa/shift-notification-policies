Privacy Policy

Effective July 25, 2026

1.Who we are

Noah Schwartz ("we," "us") operates a text-message shift-scheduling notification service (the "Service") for a small restaurant team. This policy describes the information we collect through the Service and how we use it.

2.Information we collect

  • Contact information: your name and mobile phone number, provided when you enroll.
  • Scheduling information: shifts, availability, position qualifications, and hours worked, used to operate the Service.
  • Message data: the content of text messages you send to the Service and delivery metadata (timestamps, delivery status).

3.How we use information

We use this information solely to operate the Service: sending shift-coverage alerts and offers, confirming schedule changes, responding to your messages, and keeping an accurate record of scheduling communications. We do not use it for advertising.

4.Text messaging program

The Service sends operational scheduling messages. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help and STOP to cancel at any time. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of employment or of any purchase.

5.Mobile information and sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

6.Service providers

Messages are delivered through telecommunications service providers acting on our behalf (for example, our messaging platform and carriers). These providers process information only as needed to deliver the Service and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

7.Data retention

We retain scheduling and message records for as long as needed for legitimate operational and record-keeping purposes, after which they are deleted or de-identified.

8.Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9.Your choices

You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. To review, correct, or request deletion of your information, contact us at noahschwa@gmail.com.

10.Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above reflects the latest revision; material changes will be communicated through the Service.
